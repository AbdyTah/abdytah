Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How fast is turnaround?

Depends on scope, but most short-form edits are delivered within 2–4 days. I'll confirm a clear timeline before starting.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on length, complexity, and turnaround. Message me with your project and I'll quote a fair price upfront.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are part of every project — you review the first cut and request changes. We'll agree on the number of revision rounds when I confirm scope, before I start.

What do you need from me to start?

Your raw footage or brief, the platform it's for, and any reference videos or brand guidelines you have.

Do you only work with clients in Ethiopia?

No — I work with creators and brands anywhere; everything is handled remotely over WhatsApp/Telegram.
